Steel-Panel Privacy Fence with walls

Steel-panel are precision corrugated steel profiles chosen for strength and clean architectural lines. We frame each Fence with heavy posts set in concrete and properly engineered rails so the finished installation is rigid, wind-rated, and visually precise. Available options include:

  • Heights from 5 feet upward
  • Galvanized or custom-painted finishes in refined Southwest tones
  • Architectural top caps, corner details, and base channels
  • Integration with pipe corrals, kennels, or shade structures
  • Matching steel gates

Local fabrication in the Phoenix area ensures exact fit, controlled quality, and reliable scheduling — the same process used for our equestrian and pet installations.
